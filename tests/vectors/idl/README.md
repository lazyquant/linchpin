# IDL test vectors

The nine Anchor IDLs that Marinade's programs publish on chain, decompressed from their IDL accounts in a read-only spike on 2026-10-06 (slot ~453,859,220, Alchemy mainnet endpoint; `scripts/spike/tokenomics-probe.ts`). They are **test inputs** for the IDL parser, Borsh decoder and instruction inventory. They are not evidence: the evidence copies come from `scripts/record-contracts.ts` through the recorder (fixtures under `fixtures/marinade-contracts/`). SPL Governance publishes no Anchor IDL.
